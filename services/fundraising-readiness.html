Fundraising readiness for B2B SaaS founders.

Make the numbers, ownership story, and diligence materials defensible before investor questions arrive.

Direct answer: Fundraising readiness is the work of making the operating model, data room, cap table, and investor narrative tell the same story. A polished deck cannot compensate for definitions or ownership records that change under diligence.

What the work covers

Centripetal helps founders turn preparation into a decision system: one set of definitions, one model connected to actuals, one ownership story, and a clear explanation of the choices behind the forecast.

  • Reconcile ARR, CARR, bookings, revenue, collections, burn, and runway.
  • Pressure-test base and downside cases against hiring, pricing, collections, and financing timing.
  • Prepare a data room that answers the questions investors open first.
  • Model conversion, dilution, option-pool, and financing scenarios forward.
  • Align board reporting, model outputs, and the financing narrative.
Readiness is not a folder full of documents. It is the ability to answer the same question consistently from the deck, model, data room, and bank account.Centripetal operating principle

Where founders usually lose time

Definitions drift

ARR means one thing in the deck and another in the model. Services revenue is mixed into recurring revenue. Bookings are presented as collections. These are fixable—if found before diligence.

Ownership is modeled too late

SAFEs, notes, options, and side letters need to be understood before the term sheet conversation, not after an investor asks for the fully diluted view.

The downside is not decision-ready

A base case is not a strategy. The team needs to know which trigger changes hiring, spend, fundraising timing, or capital structure.

Frequently asked questions

What does fundraising readiness include?

Model reconciliation, data-room organization, cap-table and instrument review, downside scenarios, and a consistent financing narrative.

How early should a founder start preparing?

Before investor outreach creates urgency. Lead time depends on the condition of the model, ownership records, reporting cadence, and financing complexity.

Pressure-test the raise before the raise.

Bring the model, ownership records, or investor questions. We’ll start with what does not reconcile yet.
