ServiceEmbedded finance leadership

Fractional CFO support for B2B SaaS founders.

Senior finance leadership for the moments when the numbers need to become a decision system.

Direct answer: A fractional CFO is most useful when a SaaS company’s finance work has become too interconnected for a founder, bookkeeper, controller, or outside advisor to coordinate alone. The role is to connect cash, reporting, capital, and operating choices—then build the cadence that keeps them connected.

What the engagement is built to do

Centripetal embeds with the leadership team rather than delivering a quarterly opinion from the sidelines. The work is shaped around the decisions in front of the company: a raise, a board meeting, a cash constraint, a pricing change, a new debt facility, or the handoffs that break as the team grows.

  • Build a forecast that management can use every week.
  • Turn accounting output into board and investor reporting.
  • Model fundraising, venture debt, dilution, and runway choices.
  • Clarify ownership across close, billing, collections, treasury, and planning.
  • Give founders a senior finance partner who carries context across decisions.
The question is not “Do we need a CFO?” It is “Which financial decisions are now too important to leave disconnected?”Centripetal operating principle

Where embedded support creates leverage

Before a financing process

Reconcile definitions, pressure-test the model, prepare the data room, and make the narrative defensible before investor questions arrive.

During operating growth

Connect hiring, pricing, collections, cash, and customer concentration so growth does not create an avoidable financing problem.

After the raise

Translate new capital into an operating cadence: board reporting, scenario planning, treasury controls, and decision ownership.

Frequently asked questions

What does a fractional CFO own?

A fractional CFO connects forecasting, cash, board reporting, fundraising, capital strategy, and the ownership system behind the numbers.

When is fractional CFO support useful for a SaaS company?

When decisions exceed the current finance team's capacity: a financing process, board reporting upgrade, cash-pressure scenario, or growing set of cross-functional handoffs.
