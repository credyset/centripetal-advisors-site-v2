What We Built and Why

Content Layer

The SEO audit found that centripetaladvisors.com has strong brand and positioning but weak organic acquisition structure — no schema markup, no FAQ structure, minimal internal linking, and no blog layer. We built the content system the audit recommended:

  • 45 blog articles — built from on-disk LinkedIn post drafts, the Charles Content Library (all 16 pillars including WhatsApp material), and SEO audit recommendations
  • 14 guides and interactive tools — scorecard, diligence readiness, venture debt, treasury hygiene, CFO fit calculator, runway modeler, board deck builder
  • 6 service subpages with structured schema for search engines and AI systems
  • Testimonials page — 14 cards from 7 sources (Appwork, Leverage VC, Exponential/MagicCare AI, HealthTech CEO, plus Reinman, Harris, Vinick, Rose/Schweitzer)
  • Privacy policy mirrored from the live site

What's Outstanding

  1. Homepage visual refinement — The rebuild mirrors the primary site's sections but CSS styling isn't pixel-perfect compared to Framer's rendering. We'll continue tuning.
  2. Measurement baseline — We need Search Console data, GA4 conversion paths, and AI prompt citation results to know which pages are actually performing. Without this, further content additions risk duplication.
  3. Content quality review — The newest articles use claim-safe language and first-party material, but Charles hasn't reviewed the blog copy for voice accuracy.
  4. Referral program content — Email touchpoint copy is staged but flagged for counsel/CPA review before publication.
  5. Production migration path — If the structure is approved, we need to decide how to bring the schema, FAQ markup, blog articles, and guides into the live Framer site (or whether the GitHub site becomes a parallel content hub).
