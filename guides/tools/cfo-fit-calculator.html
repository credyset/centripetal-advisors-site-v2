Frequently asked questions

What does the fit calculator evaluate?

It considers stage, finance complexity, current team capacity, and the company's highest-priority need.

Does the result replace a finance hiring discussion?

No. The result is a starting point; the right choice depends on the work mix, decision cadence, and ownership the company needs.

When is fractional CFO support useful?

It is useful when strategic finance work matters but does not yet require a full-time daily finance leader.
