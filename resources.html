Frequently asked questions

How should a founder choose a Centripetal resource?

Start with the decision in front of you: use a guide for a structured framework, a tool for a directional self-assessment, or a field note for a focused operating question.

Are the tools and guides a substitute for advice?

No. They are directional resources designed to make assumptions and next questions visible; the right interpretation depends on the company's operating context.

Where should I start if several finance issues are connected?

Start with the resource closest to the immediate decision—cash, capital, reporting, or finance ownership—then follow the related service or guide links from there.

Have a finance question specific to your stage?

Bring the model, board question, or capital decision. We’ll start with the operating reality behind the number.
