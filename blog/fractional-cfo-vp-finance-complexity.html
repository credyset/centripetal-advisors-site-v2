Fractional CFO or VP Finance? The Decision Is Complexity, Not ARR

The decision is about finance-work complexity and operating ownership: use a fractional CFO when senior judgment is needed across a defined cadence, and consider an in-house VP Finance when the company needs a daily owner for the finance function.

Work Mix

The first question is what the role actually requires. Monthly close and reporting, board materials, investor updates, cash management, modeling, and strategic analysis may need senior judgment without requiring one person to own every daily finance task. A fractional CFO can own that judgment layer while a bookkeeper or controller owns the operating workflows.

Reporting Complexity

The inflection point is not revenue. It is reporting complexity and ownership load. Multiple entities, international operations, complex revenue recognition, covenant compliance, or a daily cross-functional cadence can justify a VP Finance when the work requires one accountable owner across the function.

The Ownership Comparison

The difference is not only cost. It is ownership: a fractional CFO provides embedded senior judgment around a defined operating cadence, while a VP Finance owns the daily finance function and its cross-functional follow-through.

When to Transition

The handoff becomes worth considering when the company needs someone managing a growing finance team, owning daily cross-functional coordination, and carrying the operating cadence between board cycles. The timing varies with complexity and ownership load, not with a fixed ARR milestone.

The decision is not only about what you can afford. It is about the ownership the work requires. Match the structure to the operating cadence and complexity in front of the business, not to a revenue benchmark that may not apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should a SaaS company hire a VP Finance instead of a fractional CFO?
When the company needs a daily owner for a growing finance team, multi-entity reporting, covenant compliance, and recurring cross-functional operating cadence across the finance function.
How does a fractional CFO differ from a VP Finance?
An embedded fractional CFO provides senior judgment around a defined operating cadence. A VP Finance owns the daily finance function and its cross-functional follow-through.
Can a fractional CFO handle board reporting and investor relations?
Yes. Depending on the engagement, that work can include board reporting, investor updates, fundraising models, and cap-table coordination for Seed and Series A companies.
Direct answer: The decision between a fractional CFO and a VP Finance turns on finance-work complexity, operating ownership, and the cadence the company actually needs — not a revenue milestone.
