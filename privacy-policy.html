Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Centripetal Advisors, LLC ("Centripetal," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information obtained through our website and services.

Information We Collect

A. Personal Information

  • Name, company name, job title
  • Email address, phone number
  • Billing address, business address

B. Business and Financial Information

For clients and prospects:

  • Financial statements, forecasts, and models
  • Cap tables, equity structure documentation
  • Bank account details (for treasury support)
  • Investor and fundraising documentation

C. Website Usage Data

When you visit our website, we may collect:

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and links clicked
  • Time spent on the website
  • Cookies and analytics data via tools like Google Analytics

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide financial modeling, capital advisory, and strategic CFO services
  • Respond to inquiries and schedule consultations
  • Conduct internal business operations and improve our service offerings
  • Send marketing or thought leadership communications (with opt-out options)
  • Comply with legal obligations or respond to regulatory inquiries

Information Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

We may share your information with:

  • Authorized Service Providers who assist with bookkeeping, legal compliance, cloud storage, or technology tools integral to delivering our services
  • Regulatory or Legal Authorities when required to comply with applicable laws, subpoenas, or enforcement actions
  • Investors or Financial Counterparties, but only when explicitly authorized by you as part of a capital raising or transaction support process

All third-party partners are subject to confidentiality obligations consistent with this policy.

Data Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. These include:

  • Access controls and password-protected systems
  • Data encryption in transit and at rest for sensitive documents
  • Limited internal access based on role necessity

While no system is completely secure, we take all reasonable precautions to mitigate the risk of unauthorized access or disclosure.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience and analyze site traffic. You may adjust your browser settings to decline cookies, though this may affect certain site features.

We may also use third-party services such as Google Analytics to track aggregated, non-personal site usage data.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of inaccurate or outdated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw consent for data processing, where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at: privacy@centripetaladvisors.com

Data Retention

We retain personal and business data for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, or contractual obligations
  • Maintain historical financial records for audit or reference

Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Third-Party Links

Our website or materials may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such sites.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will revise the "Last Updated" date and post the new policy on our website. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact:

Centripetal Advisors, LLC
privacy@centripetaladvisors.com
