Frequently asked questions

What does an embedded fractional CFO engagement look like?

It typically involves 10-20 hours per week of senior financial work: board reporting, cash management, fundraising support, financial modeling, and capital structure decisions. The CFO operates as a member of the leadership team, not an outside advisor.

When should a SaaS founder consider a fractional CFO?

When financial decisions have become too interconnected for the founder, bookkeeper, or controller to coordinate alone — and before the company needs a full-time finance executive.

How long do fractional CFO engagements typically last?

At Centripetal, the average engagement lasts 2.5 years. The work spans fundraises, board transitions, and growth inflections — it is not project-based.
