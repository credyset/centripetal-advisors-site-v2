Before You Reach Out

What should I include when I reach out?

Start with the finance question creating the most pressure, such as cash planning, fundraising readiness, board reporting, finance operations, or whether the current finance support is enough.

What happens after I send the note?

The first conversation is a practical discussion of the situation and the next useful step. We always start out with free advice, and if there is a relationship and we want to work together, we will probably find a way.

Do you work with companies that already have finance support?

Yes. Many founders already have a bookkeeper, controller, internal operator, or finance process in place. The question is usually whether the work mix now needs embedded financial strategy and clearer ownership.

Do I need a complete finance plan before reaching out?

No. Bring the finance question creating the most pressure and whatever current materials help explain it. The first conversation is about clarifying the situation and identifying the next useful step.
